1 · Headphones 2 · Mic test 3 · Play along

  • 1Put headphones on. If the mic hears the metronome it scores the click as if you had played it.
  • 2Listen to the first four clicks without playing. That is the microphone test — nothing is being measured about you yet.
  • 3A 4-3-2-1 count-in tells you when to start, then play one note on every click — same string, no strumming. Those notes measure two things at once: your delay, and how readily your instrument needs to be listened for.
